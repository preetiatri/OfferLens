Privacy Policy for OfferLens

Last Updated: July 23, 2026

OfferLens ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). OfferLens is an independent offer-discovery tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any bank, card issuer, or merchant named or displayed in the App.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Data

Most users interact with the App without creating a traditional account (we use anonymous sign-in by default). Data associated with your app instance may include:

  • Device/Advertising Identifiers: Used by AdMob for ad delivery and, where enabled by your device settings, ad personalization.
  • User Preferences: Selected banks and payment types to personalize offer discovery.

B. Usage & Diagnostic Data

We automatically collect information when you access the App:

  • Log Information: Details about how you use the App, including offer views and interaction times.
  • Crash & Diagnostic Data: Via Google Firebase Crashlytics, we collect crash reports, stack traces, and basic device information (device model, OS version) to identify and fix bugs. This collection begins automatically when the App starts, before any other screen is shown, so that we can diagnose crashes that happen during startup.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information collected about you via the App to:

  • Personalize your experience by showing offers matching your selected banks/cards.
  • Provide and maintain the Savings Calculator functionality.
  • Deliver advertisements (banners and interstitials) for non-premium users.
  • Diagnose and fix crashes and technical issues.
  • Perform analytics to improve App performance and user experience.

3. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Google Firebase (authentication, database, crash reporting, analytics) and Google AdMob (advertisements).
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics

5. Security & Data Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • SQLCipher Encryption: Your local device cache is encrypted.
  • Firestore Security Rules: We restrict access to personal data so that you can only read and modify your own information.
  • Anonymous Sign-In: We prioritize privacy by allowing you to use the App without mandatory personal identification.

6. Your Rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

If you are located in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct or update inaccurate data.
  • Request erasure of your data and account.
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing (e.g., personalized ads, via your device's ad settings).

You can delete your locally-stored preferences and cloud-stored account data directly from the App via Profile > Delete My Data. This permanently removes your saved preferences and anonymous account from our systems. For any other request, contact us using the details in Section 8.

7. Merchant Rights & Opt-Out

OfferLens displays offer information about banks, card issuers, and merchants for informational purposes only, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. We respect the preferences of merchants and banks. Offer data is currently added and reviewed manually by our team; if we introduce any automated data collection in the future, we will honor robots.txt and applicable scraping restrictions.

  • Merchant Opt-Out: If you are a merchant and wish to have your offers removed, please use our Merchant Opt-Out Form.

8. Contact Us / Grievance Officer

For questions about this Privacy Policy, or to exercise your data rights under Section 6, please contact:

Grievance Officer: OfferLens Support Team
Email: contact@asadigital.co.in
Response Time: We aim to acknowledge requests within 7 business days.
